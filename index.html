      Privacy Policy

      Effective 14 October 2026. Bahi is owned and run by , Hyderabad, India ("we").

      1. What we collect

      • Account: your email, your name and phone number (if you add them), and your Google name if you sign in with Google.
      • Shop data you enter: customer names, phone numbers, notes, purchases, bills and shop details.
      • On your device: theme, currency, sort order, shop details for bills, bill counters and a short log of bills you sent.

      2. Why we use it

      To sign you in with one-time email links, show your ledger, make bills and help you when you contact support. We do not sell your data, show ads or profile you.

      3. Where it is stored

      Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Google processes it for us and servers may be outside India. Security rules let only your verified account read your data. We do not browse your ledger; we may look only if you ask for support or the law requires it.

      4. Your customers' data

      You decide what to store about your customers. Please get their permission to keep their phone number and to send them bills, and keep only what you need.

      5. Sending bills

      When you tap WhatsApp or SMS, the bill text is handed to that app on your phone. We do not see it, and delivery is covered by their policies.

      6. Your rights

      Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can access, correct, download and erase your data and withdraw consent. Download everything from Profile → Your data. Delete your account and all data from Profile → Account → Delete account.

      7. How long we keep it

      Until you delete your account. Short-lived backups at Google may take a little longer to clear.

      8. Security

      Data is encrypted in transit and only verified emails can sign in. Keep your email account safe because login links go there. No system is perfectly secure.

      9. Children

      The app is for adults running a business, not for people under 18.

      10. Changes and grievances

      We will post changes here with a new date. For questions or complaints write to at . We reply within 30 days.
